## 📊 Core Mathematical Metrics

The system monitors three macro-indicators to evaluate human evolution without relying on outdated market metrics (like GDP):

### 1. Cognitive Complexity Index (CCI)
Measures the computational depth and Shannon entropy ($H$) of the conceptual and scientific output ($X$) generated by the species:
$$CCI = H(X) \cdot (1 + \log_{10}(|X|))$$

### 2. Systemic Empathy Index (SEI)
Measures the mitigation of destructive behavioral friction within the population. It is inversely proportional to the spectral radius ($\rho$) of the conflict interaction matrix $M$:
$$SEI = \frac{1}{1 + \rho(M)}$$

### 3. Entropy Gap (EG)
The thermodynamic efficiency of the civilization, balancing useful intelligence production against metabolic and infrastructural energy overhead:
$$EG = \frac{CCI_{\text{output}}}{\ln(1 + E_{\text{input}})}$$
